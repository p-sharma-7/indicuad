Regulations”)– Receipt of Work order
Pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, we are pleased to inform you
that Mayasheel Ventures Limited (“MVL”) has received a significant work order under the
Border Infrastructure segment.
In this regard, we are pleased to inform that:

Central Public Works Department (CPWD) ICBPZ, Leh, have awarded work related to
Construction of 135 feet earthen bundh, metalled road and Naka cum machan fighting
bunkers along existing fencing along 179 km of international border in Jammu sector
along Indo–Pakistan Border. (SH: Construction of earthen bundh, lateral road, ditch and
Naka cum machan fighting bunkers including water supply, sanitary installation, internal
electrical installation in District Jammu, Samba & Kathua under Jammu sector on EPC
Mode–Package-II, BOP Chinaz to BOP Karotna Khurd) to mayasheel Ventures Limited.
The above work has been allotted based on the technical and financial evaluation conducted by
the competent authority. The Company considers this order as part of its ongoing strategy to
expand its presence in the Border Infrastructure and EPC Civil Works segment.
The disclosure as required under SEBI Listing Regulations read with SEBI Circular No.
SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD2/CIR/P/0155 dated November 11, 2024 is attached herewith and marked
as Annexure A to this letter.
This intimation is also being made available on the website of the Company at
https://mayasheelventures.com/
You are requested to kindly take the same on record.
