
Nature of Contract: EPC (Engineering, Procurement and Construction)

Order Value: ₹3,06,53,00,000/- (Rupees Three Hundred Six Crore Fifty-Three
Lakh Only), excluding GST

Mode of Award: Through tender process; accepted as lowest evaluated
responsive bid

Time Period: 36 months
The bid for the aforesaid project was submitted in a Joint Venture (JV) with M/s JSP
Projects Pvt. Ltd., wherein Mayasheel Ventures Limited is the Lead Member. The

in accordance with the terms of the JV agreement.
