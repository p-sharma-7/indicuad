4. Service Scope
4.1 Introduction
The Operation and Maintenance of Electrical System/ Electrical Installation and its machines and equipment are required by the buyers for smooth working and performance of
the Electrical System/ Electrical Installation and its machines and equipment. The work will comprise of regular maintenance/preventive maintenance of various electrical
equipments and machines as mentioned by the buyer in the bid documents.
The operations of Electrical System/ Electrical Installation and its machines and equipment will be as per the requirement of buyer and might be required round-the-clock. Regular
checking of parameters is required for the smooth operation of the plant. All measures should be taken to operate the plant satisfactorily.
4.2 Scope of Work
i. The scope of work includes operation (as applicable), servicing, repairing and maintenance of Electrical System/ Electrical Installation.
ii. Responsibility to maintain Electrical System/ Electrical Installation in a healthy state all the time, to plan and undertake preventive maintenance of Electrical System/ Electrical
Installation at regular intervals and attend routine maintenance activity every day and other additional required duties mentioned in the bid.
iii. Adherence to various obligations as mentioned under service provider obligations
